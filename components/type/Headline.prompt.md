Fraunces 400 headline in warm ivory, with one gold-bright italic phrase via <Em>.

```jsx
<Headline size="l">Dark ground, <Em>gold light.</Em></Headline>
<Headline size="title">Short ingredient list. <Em>Long game.</Em></Headline>
```

- Exactly one <Em> per headline, usually the second clause.
- Sentence case, ends with a full stop.
