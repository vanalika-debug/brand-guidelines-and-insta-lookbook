Square boxed card (panel fill, hairline border) for parts, beliefs, filters and channels.

```jsx
<Panel eyebrow="04 · Promise" title={<>Short ingredient list. <Em>Long game.</Em></>} active>
  Single-origin chocolate you can read, enjoy daily, and stay with for decades.
</Panel>
```

- Usually in rows of 3 or 4 with equal widths. At most one active panel per row.
- No radius, no shadow.
