En-dash bullet list, optionally titled; items can carry a bold lead-in phrase.

```jsx
<DashList title="What stays out" items={['Refined sugar', 'Palm oil and vegetable fats', 'Maida']} />
<DashList items={[{ lead: 'Ground and light.', text: 'Near-black ground, one warm gold key light from the upper left.' }]} />
```
