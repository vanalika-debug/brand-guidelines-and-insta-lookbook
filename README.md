# Velour Design System

Velour (Velour Luxury Dark Chocolates Pvt. Ltd.) is an Indian bean-to-bar dark chocolate brand: single-origin Criollo from Idukki, Kerala, stone-ground slowly, dark only (60% and up), with short ingredient lists. Founded 2020 in the founder's home kitchen, incorporated 2024. Founder Vanalika Dhakre Nagpal owns product, brand and voice; co-founder Aman Nagpal owns growth, channels and finance. Sells D2C via velourchocolates.com (Shopify), Instagram, WhatsApp ("The Velour Table") and corporate/wedding gifting.

Positioning (internal v2): *the everyday longevity food of Indian chocolate*. Enemy: **The Fine Print**. Audience: those who check every back label, starting with women managing PCOS and hormonal health.

## CONTENT FUNDAMENTALS

**Voice: Warm. Editorial. Unhurried. Precise.**
- **Warm** — signs off as people, never a brand: "With warmth, the Velour team." Customer replies name a person.
- **Editorial** — considered sentences, not ad copy. Full sentences broken deliberately by em dashes and fragments: "Short ingredient list. Long game."
- **Unhurried** — no exclamation points, ever. No urgency, no "limited time", no countdowns, no discounts as a message.
- **Precise** — names the craft: *slow-ground, single-origin, bean-to-bar, small-batch, stone-ground, Criollo, Idukki*. Never "premium" or "luxury" without a reason attached.

**Person.** "We" for Velour, "you" for the reader; founder quotes in first person, always verbatim and attributed ("Vanalika Dhakre Nagpal · The Glitz Media, 2025").

**Casing.** Sentence case for headlines, ending in a full stop. Mono labels in UPPERCASE with wide tracking. Levels chained with " · " and " — ": `03 · TENSION — IN THE WORLD`. Page numbers zero-padded: `02 / 14`. British/Indian spelling (colour, flavour). Rupee figures in Indian notation (₹10,09,001; ₹10.09 L).

**Headline pattern.** Two beats; the second (the emotional phrase) set in gold-bright italic. "Dark ground, *gold light.*" · "Belief is what you *give up.*" · "The promise *is already being bought.*"

**Lines.** Master: "The true luxury lies in good health." Brand: "Short ingredient list. Long game." Emotional: "A small ritual for a long life." Also: "Read the label. Then trust it." · "Nothing on the label we wouldn't say out loud." · "Good health, dressed as indulgence."

**Reach for:** slow-ground, single-origin, bean-to-bar · read the label, back label, ingredient · ritual, daily, small, long game · gifted, a box built for the moment.
**Avoid:** premium/luxury (alone) · guilt-free as a headline, cheat, detox · miracle, superfood, hack · deal, flash, last chance.

**Claims.** Everything printed on Velour packaging is accurate and cleared (confirmed by Velour, 13 Oct 2026). Cleared: PCOS-friendly, PCOS & thyroid friendly, hormonal-friendly, hormonal balancing, low GI index (London Almond), protein rich, fibre rich, low fat (Florentine), no refined sugar, sugar-free (stevia range), no maida, no butter, no palm oil, vegan *by product* (20 of 22, incl. Florentine), single-origin Criollo, organically grown Indian cacao, lab tested, hygiene verified, good source of protein, "cocoa is a flavanol-rich food". Use a claim only on the product that carries it. Still never: clinically proven, superfood, immunity booster, miracle, any prevent/treat/cure, "vegan" for the whole range, "longevity food" as fact.

**Captions.** Line 1: a scene-setting story line ending on an ellipsis ("In the hills of Idukki, a hornbill keeps watch over the cacao…"). Line 2: one plain, cleared product fact. Close: a person's name or "With warmth, the Velour team" (Menagerie posts: "Drawn in the Indian miniature tradition."). Data always carries a source line.

**Emoji:** none. The only glyph-icons in the source are typographic: `–` en-dash bullets, `·` separators, `☀` marking key light in schematics.

## VISUAL FOUNDATIONS

**Colour — "Dark ground, gold light."** Ground 60% · Ink 30% · Gold 10%. Ground `#15100C` (warm near-black, never cool), Ground 2 `#1D1610`, Panel `#221A13`. Ink `#F3E8D6` (warm ivory, never pure white), Ink Soft `#C9B89C`, Ink Faint `#8A7860`. Gold `#C89F5B` for eyebrows, rules, icons; Gold Bright `#E7C884` for emphasis and italics. Gold is a light, not a fill — never large gold areas. One Colour of the Year accent: 2026 deep teal `#00594C`, ribbon/lining/sticker only, ≤10%, never replacing gold. There is no light theme; never plain white grounds.

**Type — four voices, one register.** Alex Brush sets the word "Velour" (and only that word) wherever the master PNG can't be used: emails, WhatsApp headers, Instagram, signatures. Gold, never below 20px, never tracked, never with other words in the script. The full lockup with "Luxury Dark Chocolates" stays the master PNG. Headlines: Fraunces 400–600, 34–96px, LH 1.1–1.18, gold-bright italic for the one emotional phrase. Body: Archivo 300 (600 for bold lead-ins), 15–16px screen / 10.5–13px print, LH 1.55–1.7. Labels: JetBrains Mono uppercase, tracking .24–.5em — eyebrows, page numbers, footers, tags; never body. Word-spacing never adjusted; tighter tracking as type grows. *(The live website uses a different serif pairing — see Website below.)*

**Backgrounds.** A warm near-black ground with a soft radial lift (Ground → Ground 2, brighter upper area) and a faint paper grain across the frame. No patterns, no busy gradients. Imagery (photography or Menagerie art) carries colour; layout does not.

**Layout.** Generous negative space, content top-aligned and left-aligned. Deck pages: 6.9% side margins, running mono footer ("VELOUR — NARRATIVE" left, "02 / 14" right). Short gold rule (≈40px screen / 86px at 1920) after headlines. Content in rows of 3–4 equal panels, or two-column text + list/quote. Tables are hairline-ruled with mono row labels; the last ("Velour way") column brighter.

**Cards/panels.** Square corners (radius 0), Panel fill, 1px hairline border in gold at ~22%. The one key panel in a row gets a full gold border. No shadows, no left-border accents, no rounded containers.

**Borders & rules.** Hairlines only: panel borders `--velour-line`, table/row rules `--velour-line-soft`, stat blocks sit under a hairline. Quotes use a 1.5–2px gold left rule.

**Shadows & depth.** None in layout. Depth comes from light: one warm gold key light from the upper left, real falloff, gold rim-light on the product. In imagery, soft contact shadows only — never hard cut-out edges.

**Transparency & blur.** Not used in layout. Transparency only in the logo PNG and hairline borders.

**Imagery — photography.** Moody dark, gold light, product always the brightest point. Surfaces: dark marble with gold veining, velvet, dark wood, linen; maroon silk with gold zari for festive. Props sparingly: cacao pods, almonds, figs, Medjool dates, coffee beans, dried flowers; at festivals a brass diya, marigold, saffron. Two or three tones plus one gold accent; restrained grade; tactile surfaces. **No text, badges, stickers or discounts on images** — overlays added afterwards in layout, logo composited from the master file. Formats: 1:1 Shopify, 4:5 (1080×1350) social with a calm upper third.

**Imagery — The Menagerie (from 15 Oct 2026).** Indian miniature painting (Mughal, Rajput, Company school): fine brush, jewel colour, burnished gold, matte gouache with grain. Arched frame with a thin gold keyline (echoing the arched windows on the Velour lid). One animal, one real product photograph in front, one idea. Four colour worlds, each a pair: Night (black + gold), Green (green + gold), Blush (pink + gold), Cream (cream + gold). AI is allowed for backgrounds only; product, packaging, logo and lettering are always real. No sacred imagery. The 20 reference scenes in `assets/menagerie/` are a **mood bank only** — several contain inaccurate or AI-garbled packaging.

**Packaging truth.** See `PACKAGING-TRUTH.md` (supersedes the summary previously here). Never redrawn, re-lettered or recoloured. Bars: matte black carton, gold-foil script, one illustrated animal (Noir 85% macaw, Sugar Free 90% lion). Tins: brushed gold lid, toile wrap (blue London for London Almond, sepia safari toile on cream or metallic gold for Florentine), brown rounded label with gold keyline. Gift box: cream board with gold-foil windows, fountain and birds. Ribbon: brown grosgrain with gold "Velour – Luxury Dark Chocolates". Sachets: mauve-rose film, silver seal. "Velour" script embossed in every bar segment and on the chocolate seal (florentines, Nama). Claims on existing labels are not automatically cleared — see flags in `PACKAGING-TRUTH.md`.

**Motion (decided).** Unhurried, like lifting a lid. Everything eases out on `--ease-velour`; nothing bounces, overshoots, spins, flashes or shakes. Content reveals: fade + 12px rise, 900ms, staggered 120ms. Hero imagery drifts 1.00→1.04 over 12s. Section changes crossfade 600ms. Video/Reels: no shot under 2.5s, crossfades 600–900ms, hold the product on its brightest frame; reveals in stages (ribbon → sleeve → gold light → product). Reduced motion: opacity only, 200ms.

**Hover / press / focus (decided).** 360ms colour and border shifts only — no lift, no shadow, no scale on controls. Text links: underline draws in from the left (480ms); colour gold-bright → ivory on dark, green → ink on cream. Solid buttons swap fill and text (green ↔ cream). Outline buttons fill. Panels/cards brighten their border to full gold; product images inside drift to 1.03 over 1.2s. Press: opacity .86, 120ms. Focus: 1px gold outline, 3px offset. Wishlist heart fills gold, no pop.

**Website (velourchocolates.com, live, Oct 2026).** The site runs a *light* register that differs from the deck system: cream ground `#F6F2EA` / `#EDE6D9`, forest green `#17362D` for bands, buttons and active pills, ink `#231F1B`, body `#6E655A`, dark gold `#8A6A1E` for eyebrows on cream, gold `#C9A94F` on green, gold rule `#B5923D`. Display serif looks like Cormorant Garamond; body and letter-spaced caps look like Lora (identified by eye). Square buttons with tracked caps; pill-shaped filter chips and trust badges ("LAB TESTED", "HYGIENE VERIFIED"). Tokens in `tokens/web.css`; screenshots in `assets/reference/`. Customer-facing email and WhatsApp follow the website register.

## ICONOGRAPHY
The sources contain **no icon set, icon font or SVG icons**, and the brand explicitly has **no icon or monogram** ("Invent an icon or monogram. None exists."). Iconography is typographic: en-dash `–` list markers, middot `·` separators, the gold short rule, and `☀` to mark key light in photo schematics. No emoji. If a future surface truly needs icons, use a thin-stroke line set in Gold at small size and flag it as an addition — none is included here.

**Logo.** `assets/logo/velour-logo-master.png` — the master wordmark ("Velour" script with "Luxury Dark Chocolates" subline), transparent PNG, gold-to-pink gradient fixed, extracted from the decks at 900×377. Place on the dark ground; clearspace = subline height; min 120px wide; never recolour or recreate in type.
