Part 4 · Decision filters

Six questions. Ask them before it ships.

1 · True
Would we say it out loud?
Is it accurate, specific to this product, and cleared to claim?
2 · Parents
Would we give it to our own parents?
The founding test. If not, it doesn't ship.
3 · Craft
Did we choose slow over fast?
Craft over scale: make less, better.
4 · Restraint
Is it quiet?
Moody, gold-lit, nothing shouting.
5 · Ours
Is the point of view Indian?
Nothing borrowed, nothing imported for show.
6 · Long game
Does it earn the second order?
Not just the first sale. Trust protected over margin.
