The story in one breath

Velour began in 2020 in a home kitchen, because the chocolate on the shelf wasn't clean enough for the founder's parents. Velour believes you should only make what you'd give your own parents — so it makes dark chocolate only, from Criollo grown in Idukki, ground slowly, with a short ingredient list you can read. The promise is simple: short ingredient list, long game.
