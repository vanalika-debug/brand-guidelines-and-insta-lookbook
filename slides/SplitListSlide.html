02 · Belief — In practice

Belief is what you give up.

In 2024, Velour began with a full range and chose a narrower one. Dark chocolate only. Never milk or white. Never below 60% cacao.

"I let everything else go." The focus is the point: the fewer things Velour makes, the better each one can be — and the shorter each label gets.

What stays out
  • Milk chocolate and white chocolate
  • Refined sugar
  • Palm oil and vegetable fats
  • Maida
What goes in
  • Criollo cacao, organically grown in Idukki, Kerala
  • Sunflower lecithin, a vegan emulsifier
  • Real inclusions: almonds, hazelnuts, espresso, fig
