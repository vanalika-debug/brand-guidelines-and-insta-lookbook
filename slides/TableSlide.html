At every step, there is a shortcut.

The industrial shortcut
The Velour way
Harvest
Bulk beans bought as a commodity, no named origin.
Criollo, organically grown in Idukki, Kerala.
Ferment
Rushed or skipped, leaving beans flat and bitter.
Several days in their own fruit pulp, where flavour begins.
Roast
Dark, heavy roast that hides the taste of poor beans.
A gentle roast that keeps Criollo's delicate notes.
Grind
Fast, hot milling that cooks off the aroma.
Stone-ground slowly and cool, up to three days.
Finish
Palm oil and vegetable fats, to make it cheaper.
Tempered for a clean snap and gloss. No palm oil.
Criollo is under 5% of the world's cacao (as stated on velourchocolates.com). Source: Our Cacao page.
